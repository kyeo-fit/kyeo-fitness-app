# KYEO Fitness App

KYEO (Keep Your Eyes Open) is a next-generation fitness application focused on discipline, performance, and real long-term progress.

## Vision
We are building one of the most impactful fitness apps on the market — not just for tracking, but for transforming mindset, discipline, and consistency.

## Problem
Most fitness apps focus only on tracking workouts, but fail to keep users consistent and motivated long-term.

## Solution
KYEO combines performance tracking with mindset development and a built-in AI assistant.

## Key Features
- Built-in AI assistant called KAI
- Personalized training guidance
- Motivation & discipline system
- Progress and performance tracking
